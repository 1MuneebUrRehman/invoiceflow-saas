# InvoiceFlow — Product Overview (Client Edition)

> A plain-language overview you can present to a client or prospect. No jargon — just
> what the product does, why it matters, and what they get.

---

## What is InvoiceFlow?

InvoiceFlow is an online invoicing platform built for **freelancers and small agencies**.
It takes care of the entire "getting paid" workflow: you create a professional invoice in
minutes, your client receives it by email with a secure payment link, they pay online by
card, and the system automatically follows up on late payments — so you never have to
write another awkward reminder email.

## The Problem It Solves

Small businesses lose real money to late payments, and they lose real hours every month
creating invoices, sending follow-ups, and figuring out who has paid. InvoiceFlow puts
clients, invoices, payments, and reminders in one place, and automates the chasing —
**getting paid stops being a part-time job.**

## What You Can Do With It

### 🗂 Manage your clients
Keep all your clients in one place — contact details, company, billing address, preferred
currency, and private notes. Find anyone instantly with search. Remove a client and
nothing is truly lost — records are archived safely rather than wiped.

InvoiceFlow supports **24+ currencies** (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, INR, PKR, AED, and
more), so you can bill clients in whatever currency they expect.

### 🧾 Create professional invoices in minutes
- Add line items with quantities and prices; taxes and discounts are calculated for you.
- Every invoice gets a clean sequential number like **INV-2026-0001** — automatic, no
  duplicates, no manual bookkeeping.
- Save as a draft, review, then send when ready (with a confirmation step so nothing
  goes out by accident).

### 🎨 Your brand on every invoice
Upload your business logo once — it appears on every invoice PDF and email you send.
Clients see *your* brand, not ours.

### 📧 Send with one click
Hit "Send" and your client receives a polished email with the invoice PDF attached and a
secure personal payment link. No accounts, no passwords — they just click and view.

### 💳 Get paid online
Clients pay by card through **Stripe**, the same payment processor trusted by millions of
businesses. The moment they pay, the invoice is automatically marked as paid and a
receipt goes out — nothing for you to reconcile by hand. You can also record offline
payments (bank transfer, cash) manually.

### ⏰ Automatic late-payment reminders
If an invoice goes past its due date, InvoiceFlow marks it overdue and **automatically
emails polite reminders at 3, 7, and 14 days late**. Each reminder is sent exactly once —
your clients are nudged, never spammed. This is the feature that gets you paid faster
without lifting a finger.

### 📊 See your business at a glance
The dashboard shows what matters:
- **Outstanding balance** — how much money is owed to you right now
- **Revenue this month** — what's actually been paid
- **Overdue invoices** — what needs attention
- A revenue chart and your most recent invoices

### 👥 Team workspaces
Each business gets its own private workspace. Your data is completely separate from every
other customer's. Owners control the workspace; team members can be added with their own
logins.

### 🔌 Connect your own tools (API)
For clients with developers or other software (accounting tools, CRMs, internal apps),
InvoiceFlow offers a secure API: create clients and invoices, send them, and read payment
data programmatically. API keys are created and revoked from the Settings page.

### 🔐 Security built in
- Invoice links are tamper-proof and impossible to guess.
- Card details never touch our servers — payment is handled entirely by Stripe.
- Email verification, password reset, and rate-limited logins protect every account.

## How a Typical Invoice Flows

1. **Create** — pick a client, add line items, set the due date. Totals and tax are
   calculated automatically.
2. **Send** — your client gets a branded email with the PDF and a secure "View & Pay" link.
3. **Get paid** — they pay by card; the invoice flips to *Paid* instantly and a receipt
   is sent.
4. **Or let it chase** — if they don't pay, reminders go out automatically at 3, 7, and
   14 days overdue until they do.
